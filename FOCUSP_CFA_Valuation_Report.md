## 2. Top-Down Macro & Industry Analysis

### 2.1 Macro Environment (Malaysia)
- **Interest Rate Environment:** The 10-year Malaysian Government Securities (MGS) yield sits at 3.59% (April 2026), reflecting a relatively stable inflation environment and supportive monetary policy by BNM. 
- **Consumer Sentiment:** Resilient domestic consumption remains intact, driven by stable employment and targeted government stimulus, which indirectly benefits essential discretionary retail like eyewear.

### 2.2 Sector Outlook (Retail - Optical & F&B)
- **Optical Segment (Cash Cow):** Eyewear displays characteristics of consumer staples. Aging demographics, increasing screen-time driving myopia, and premiumization via services like 360 APEC provide structural tailwinds.
- **F&B Segment (Wildcard):** The bakery segment (Komugi) has dragged historical margins but shows stabilization into 2024 via corporate B2B contracts (e.g., supplying major convenience chains) and central kitchen optimization.

## 3. Cost of Capital Estimation

Applying the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) to derive the Cost of Equity ($K_e$):

*   **Risk-Free Rate ($R_f$):** 3.59% (10Y MGS)
*   **Equity Risk Premium (ERP):** 5.06% (Malaysia standard estimate)
*   **Beta ($\beta$):** 0.80 (Derived conservative proxy reflecting defensive nature of optical retail, adjusted against historical volatility of the F&B segment)

**Cost of Equity ($K_e$) Formula:**
$K_e = R_f + \beta \times ERP$
$K_e = 3.59\% + 0.80 \times 5.06\% = 7.64\%$

Assuming a conservative capital structure (low debt weighting typical of their operating lease-heavy model prior to recent expansions), the **Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)** is estimated at ~**7.20%**.

## 4. Absolute Valuation: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)

We employ a 2-Stage Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) approach:
*   **Base Year Earnings:** 9MFY24 Net Profit is RM 24.0m, implying a normalized annualized FY24 Net Profit of roughly RM 32.0m.
*   **Growth Phase (Years 1-5):** Factoring in steady optical expansion and Komugi's margin recovery, we assume a conservative 8% CAGR in FCFE.
*   **Terminal Value:** Assumed stable terminal growth rate ($g$) of 3.0% (GDP tracking).
*   **Discount Rate:** WACC of 7.20%.

**Valuation Mechanics:**
The high cash-generative nature of the optical business, combined with a relatively low discount rate (7.20%), yields a strong PV for terminal value. Our base case DCF yields an intrinsic equity value translating to approximately **RM 0.82 per share**. 

> [!TIP]
> Sensitivity Analysis: If the terminal growth rate shifts to 2.5%, the intrinsic value drops to ~RM 0.76. If Komugi achieves superior B2B scale unlocking 10% FCFE CAGR, value exceeds RM 0.90.
